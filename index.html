Penki Tirupathi Rao works on electrochemical energy storage. His published work covers lithium- and manganese-rich cathodes for Li-ion batteries, sodium-ion and magnesium-ion electrodes, supercapacitors and lead-carbon systems, in collaboration with groups across Israel, India, Germany and the United States.
